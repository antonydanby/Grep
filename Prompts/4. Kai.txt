# Observations
When testing I was searching through large codebases it took a matter of a number of minutes before I had found everything, but with 30 seconds I could see the files and lines I had been searching for. So what we need to add to Grep.Core is a way to stop the search cleanly and for the Search and Clear buttons to come back to enabled immediately upon stopping. 

## What we need:
- Add to Grep.Core a way to stop the search cleanly 

## When Starting the Search
- We need a Stop button and a progress bar to appear when we have started searching and to both disappear when we click Stop. 
- The stop button could go where the Clear button is now
- The progress bar should also disappear 

## When Search is Stopped
- When the Search is stopped then Search and Clear buttons to come back to enabled immediately.
- The Stop button should disappear
- The progress bar should also disappear 

## Important
- The TProgress bar should be Style := pbstMarquee. I have created one in the GrepVCL project
- All these UI changes are for both GrepVCL and GrepFMX
